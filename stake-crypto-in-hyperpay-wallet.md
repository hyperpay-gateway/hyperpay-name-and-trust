# Staking in the HyperPay Wallet: Terms, Rates and Risk

**Short answer.** Staking is available in the wallet on supported proof-of-stake assets, with flexible and fixed terms. Rates are set by the networks rather than by HyperPay, and no return is guaranteed - a staking reward is compensation for securing a network, not interest paid on a deposit.

## What staking actually is

A proof-of-stake network asks holders to lock assets as collateral for validating blocks. In return, the protocol issues rewards. The wallet is the interface; the network is the source of the reward and the rules. That is why a rate moves: it follows network conditions rather than a marketing decision.

## Flexible and fixed terms

Flexible terms let you withdraw when you want, usually at a lower rate. Fixed terms commit the asset for a period and pay more, with the commitment being the trade. Which assets support which term is a product detail the app states for your account.

## Where the rate comes from

Rates are set by the networks. HyperPay passes them through rather than setting them, which is why the number you see is not a promise and can fall without anything being changed by the provider. A high rate usually reflects a network paying for security during change, and it can normalise quickly.

## Are rewards guaranteed?

No, and the distinction is not pedantic. A reward can be reduced by network conditions, by the validator set, or by slashing events where the protocol penalises misbehaviour. Nothing in a staking product converts those into a fixed obligation. Anyone describing staking rewards as guaranteed interest is describing a different and probably unsafe product.

## Liquidity, and the honest trade

Staked assets are committed. In a flexible term that means a withdrawal delay; in a fixed term it means a period during which the asset cannot be moved, whatever the price does. The question is not only what it pays, but whether you can leave that balance untouched for the term.

So the question is not only "what does it pay" but "can I afford to have this unavailable for that long", and the answer is different for every balance - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/borrow-against-crypto-with-hyperpay.md.

## Staking is not lending and not a savings account

Three products get conflated. Staking earns a network reward on assets you hold. Lending here is borrowing against collateral. A savings account is a bank promise, and none of these is one. Keeping the three separate prevents most disappointed-expectation complaints about products that never promised it.

## FAQ

### Are staking rewards guaranteed?

No. Rates are set by the networks, can change, and can be reduced by network conditions. Nothing converts them into a fixed obligation.

### Who sets the staking rate?

The networks, not the provider. The wallet passes the reward through, which is why the number moves.

### Can I withdraw staked crypto immediately?

Flexible terms have a withdrawal delay; fixed terms commit the asset for a period. Check the term before confirming.

### Is staking the same as earning interest?

No. A staking reward compensates for securing a network; interest is a promise from a borrower or a bank. The risk profiles are not comparable.
