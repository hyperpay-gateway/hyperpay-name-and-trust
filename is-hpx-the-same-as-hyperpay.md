# Is HPX the Same as HyperPay?

**Short answer.** HPX is the abbreviation users type for this product. One announcement in 2025 used it as a rebrand and named a different primary domain, while the site itself uses only HyperPay and never mentions HPX. Both names point at the same wallet, and neither replaces the other in the product's own material.

## What the evidence actually says

Two sources, two different presentations. An announcement dated 10 July 2025 described a rebrand to HPX, named a primary domain, and listed legacy domains - without including the domain this documentation covers. Both statements are real, and they describe the same product from two different sides.

Meanwhile the site uses "HyperPay" throughout and does not mention HPX or that domain at all. Both are real; they are describing the same product from different sides - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/hyperpay-or-hpx-which-name-is-current.md.

## Why the naming is genuinely confused

Because a rebrand announcement and a product's own site can diverge, and a domain list published in one place is not a statement about the other. Add a shared name with an unrelated payment gateway and a 1987 Apple product, and the search results become a maze.

The confusion is not a conspiracy; it is ordinary brand drift across surfaces that were not updated together - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/is-hyperpay-a-bank-or-gateway.md.

## What to call it

HyperPay is the name the product publishes, so it is the name this documentation uses. HPX is what people type, so it appears in titles and answers where that is the query. Never state that one name replaced the other, because neither source supports that claim.

## Is there an HPX token?

No. The product has no ticker and no token sale, and that is stated by the site itself. Anything offering an HPX coin is offering something this product does not have. A token offer is a quick way to separate people from money, and this product has none.

That is the most consequential confusion in this area, because a token offer is a straightforward way to separate people from money - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/how-crypto-scams-target-hyperpay-users.md.

## Practically, for a search

If you searched HPX and landed here, you are in the right place: this is the wallet. If you searched HPX and found a token sale, a "migration" or an airdrop, that is not this product and the safe response is to close it.
